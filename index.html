What we do


Augmented reality

We design and develop augmented reality applications to best fit different scenarios and needs.

Geolocalized AR

Geolocalized augmented reality lets you expand the AR experience to your entire city.

Design custom routes to invite your audience to visit important historical locations in your city


AR Interior Navigation

Augmented reality indoor navigation shows your audience directions to their destination in large buildings.


Invite your audience to follow thematic paths in your museums, guiding them through specific stories and themes


Image Tracking & Object Tracking

Image tracking lets you bring augmented contents on static images, from paintings in a museum to billboards around the city.
Object tracking lets you bring augmented contents on objects in the real world, from monuments in your city to cars.

Animate historical paintings and show information on their story


Virtual reality

We use the same technologies used in the videogame industry to create realistic virtual worlds.


Immerse your audience in realistic and oustanding recreation of long lost historical buildings
